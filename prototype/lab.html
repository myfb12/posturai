Verdict

Per-measure stability

The question isn't "is the noise small" in the abstract — it's small relative to the band it has to land in. Noise is reported as ±2 SD (the shot-to-shot spread covering ~95% of shots) as a percentage of the narrowest band. Above ~50% and a photo near a boundary will flip bands on a good day and a bad one alike.

Band flips observed

The empirical answer, no statistics required: across identical postures, how many different verdicts did the app actually produce? Anything other than one band per measure means the app would tell this user their posture changed when it didn't.
